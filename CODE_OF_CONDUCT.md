# Community Conduct

Arion Volunteer is a space for careful, evidence-oriented collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- treat people with respect;
- challenge claims and designs without attacking contributors;
- state uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- protect personal, proprietary, and security-sensitive information;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, or threatening behavior.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these standards.
For private safety or conduct reports, use the repository's private security
reporting channel and clearly mark the report as a conduct concern.
