# Counterfactual Policy Review

## Use When

- a decision changes eligibility, treatment, routing, or control;
- action and inaction have different costs;
- a staged experiment may dominate an irreversible choice.

## Inputs

- current judgment and evidence boundary;
- candidate actions and affected populations;
- loss, reversibility, and observation constraints.

## Procedure

1. Define the baseline world and each candidate world.
2. State assumptions that differ across worlds.
3. Estimate intended, unintended, delayed, and displaced effects.
4. Compare false-action and false-inaction costs.
5. Prefer the smallest reversible step that creates useful evidence.
6. Define stop, rollback, and expansion conditions.

## Output

Counterfactuals, tradeoffs, staged recommendation, and decision conditions.

## Stop Conditions

Do not recommend an action when its protected boundary or rollback condition
cannot be stated.
