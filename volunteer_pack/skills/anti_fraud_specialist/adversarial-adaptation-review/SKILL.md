# Adversarial Adaptation Review

## Use When

- a control changes incentives or observability;
- a signal may be copied, hidden, or shifted;
- concentrated behavior could reflect adaptation rather than ordinary drift.

## Inputs

- control and incentive description;
- bounded evidence and affected population;
- known detection and feedback surfaces.

## Procedure

1. Identify what an adaptive participant can observe.
2. Enumerate manipulation, evasion, displacement, and collaboration paths.
3. Separate plausible paths from evidenced paths.
4. Define leading indicators and false-positive controls.
5. Record how the control itself may change the data-generating process.

## Output

Adversarial hypotheses, evidence status, evasion paths, and monitoring triggers.

## Stop Conditions

Do not attribute intent to individuals or groups without direct, governed
evidence.
