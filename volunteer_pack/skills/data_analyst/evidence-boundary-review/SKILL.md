# Evidence Boundary Review

## Use When

- a reported change or anomaly must first be confirmed;
- evidence comes from partial, selected, or changing observations;
- later roles need a stable fact boundary.

## Inputs

- reviewed data or artifacts;
- comparison definition;
- expected population, window, and grain.

## Procedure

1. Validate the input with the data-quality gate.
2. Reproduce the primary observation.
3. Check coverage, missingness, selection, and comparison symmetry.
4. Separate magnitude from explanation.
5. List viable alternative explanations and discriminating observations.

## Output

Observed facts, evidence limitations, unsupported claims, and next evidence.

## Stop Conditions

Stop short of causal or prescriptive claims when the evidence only establishes
association.
