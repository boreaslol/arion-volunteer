# Evidence Package

## Use When

- a role finishes a bounded investigation;
- work must be handed to another role or to Lead;
- a conclusion depends on evidence that must remain inspectable.

## Inputs

- observed facts and provenance;
- interpretation and competing explanations;
- limitations, missing evidence, and rejected claims.

## Procedure

1. Separate observed facts from interpretation.
2. Identify the exact scope, window, grain, and artifact references.
3. Record supporting and conflicting evidence.
4. State confidence only with its dependency and limitation.
5. Add unresolved questions, next owner, and revision trigger.

## Output

An evidence summary, provenance list, limitations, competing hypotheses, and
next handoff.

## Stop Conditions

Do not package a recommendation as fact or omit evidence that materially
weakens the finding.
