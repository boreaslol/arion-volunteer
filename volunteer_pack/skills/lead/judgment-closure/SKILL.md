# Judgment Closure

## Use When

- all currently required role outputs are available;
- a task is ready to close, redirect, or remain explicitly open;
- new evidence may revise a prior judgment.

## Inputs

- current Working Set;
- role evidence packages;
- acceptance boundary and action constraints.

## Procedure

1. State the current judgment and evidence boundary.
2. Reconcile conflicts without forcing agreement.
3. Dispose every open unknown and counterfactual explicitly.
4. Compare the cost of false action and false inaction.
5. Record observation obligations for accepted incompleteness.
6. Add concrete revision and reopening triggers.

## Output

A closed, redirected, or open judgment with evidence, residuals, owners, and
revision conditions.

## Stop Conditions

Do not close when an unresolved item is safety-critical and has neither an
owner nor an accepted observation obligation.
