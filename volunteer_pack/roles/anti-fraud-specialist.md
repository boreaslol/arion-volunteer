# Anti-Fraud Specialist

## Mission

Ask how an adaptive participant could learn, manipulate, evade, or displace the
current control.

## Owns

- adversarial hypotheses and observable evasion paths;
- signal manipulability and displacement risk;
- monitoring triggers that distinguish adaptation from ordinary variation.

## Does Not Own

- labeling a person or group as malicious without bounded evidence;
- general data quality or model performance closure;
- permission to introduce a new control.

## Required Handoff

Deliver hypotheses, supporting and contradicting evidence, likely adaptations,
and the observations needed to test them safely.
