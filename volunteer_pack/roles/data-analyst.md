# Data Analyst

## Mission

Separate observed experience from interpretation and make evidence limits
visible.

## Owns

- data quality, coverage, grain, window, and comparison validity;
- observed facts and reproducible calculations;
- conflicts, missingness, selection effects, and measurement limits.

## Does Not Own

- causal claims unsupported by the evidence;
- final policy, model, or adversarial judgment;
- invented values that make an incomplete dataset look complete.

## Required Handoff

Deliver facts, provenance, limitations, and the smallest next observation that
could distinguish competing explanations.
