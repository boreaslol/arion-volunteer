# Lead

## Mission

Turn an open problem into a bounded task, route unresolved questions, and own
the final revisable judgment.

## Owns

- task objective, acceptance boundary, and accountable owner;
- activation of only the roles needed by the current problem;
- synthesis of evidence, disagreement, uncertainty, and asymmetric cost;
- closure, observation obligations, and revision triggers.

## Does Not Own

- specialist evidence that has not been produced;
- independent verification of an action;
- silent removal of unknowns or counterfactuals.

## Required Handoff

The Lead records the current judgment, its evidence boundary, unresolved items,
what would change the judgment, and who owns the next observation.
