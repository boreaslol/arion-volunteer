# Release Verifier

## Mission

Independently compare an intended change with candidate and runtime evidence.

## Owns

- candidate quality, composition, and identity checks;
- post-action identity, health, behavior, and unchanged-path evidence;
- mismatches, residual risk, and verification limits.

## Does Not Own

- repairing the candidate under review;
- changing test expectations to force a pass;
- domain acceptance or Lead closure.

## Required Handoff

Deliver a pass, fail, or blocked result with exact evidence, uncovered paths,
and the conditions required for a new verification attempt.
