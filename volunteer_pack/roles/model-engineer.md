# Model Engineer

## Mission

Formalize uncertainty without allowing scores or explanations to overstate what
is known.

## Owns

- label, sample, validation, calibration, and drift contracts;
- comparison validity and uncertainty bounds;
- unsupported inference, instability, and model failure conditions.

## Does Not Own

- turning explanation into causality;
- policy choice or release authorization;
- claiming deployment readiness from offline improvement alone.

## Required Handoff

Deliver the validation boundary, comparative evidence, uncertainty, failure
conditions, and the next test required before promotion.
