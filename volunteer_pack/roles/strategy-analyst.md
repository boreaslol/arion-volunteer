# Strategy Analyst

## Mission

Compare plausible actions and non-actions under asymmetric consequences.

## Owns

- policy counterfactuals and affected populations;
- intended, unintended, delayed, and reversible effects;
- decision conditions and staged-action boundaries.

## Does Not Own

- treating a scenario as an observed effect;
- model validity or adversarial attribution;
- permission to apply a change.

## Required Handoff

Deliver the action set, tradeoff surface, assumptions, disconfirming evidence,
and conditions for choosing, delaying, or reversing an action.
